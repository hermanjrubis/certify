Questions

Are my template and names uploaded anywhere?

No. Certify runs in your browser. Your template, names and spreadsheet stay on your computer, and your draft is saved in this browser so you can come back to it.

Can I use more columns, like Course or Date?

Yes. Import a CSV and use any column in a text layer, for example “for completing {Course}”.

Which files can I upload, and what do I get?

Upload a PNG, JPG or WebP up to 20MB. You get one ZIP with a full-resolution PNG for each name.

Does it cost anything?

No. There's no account and no limit on the number of names.
